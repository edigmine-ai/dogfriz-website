### 3. "How to Choose the Right Size Cooling Vest for Your Dog"
*   **Target Slug:** `how-to-size-dog-cooling-vest`
*   **Meta Title:** Sizing Guide: How to Fit a Dog Cooling Vest | Dogfriz
*   **Meta Description:** Protect your pup with a perfectly fitted cooling vest. Learn how to measure your dog's chest girth, neck, and back length for optimal performance.
*   **Target Keywords:** how to size dog cooling vest, dog cooling vest sizing chart, measuring dog chest girth
*   **Article Structure:**
    *   **Introduction**: Explain that a cooling vest must fit snugly against a dog's chest and underbelly to transfer heat. If it's too loose, the evaporative layer won't cool their skin; if too tight, it restricts breathing.
    *   **Section 1: The Three Vital Measurements**:
        *   **Girth**: Measuring the widest part of the ribcage (the most important measurement).
        *   **Neck**: Measuring the base of the neck.
        *   **Length**: Measuring from the base of the neck to the tail.
    *   **Section 2: Sizing by Breed Archetype**: Compare fit requirements for broad-chested breeds (e.g., French Bulldogs, Pugs) vs. deep-chested breeds (e.g., Greyhounds) vs. long breeds (e.g., Dachshunds).
    *   **Section 3: How to Test the Fit**: The "two-finger" rule under straps and monitoring shoulder mobility.

### 4. "5 Myths About Dogs and Hot Weather, Busted!"
*   **Target Slug:** `myths-dogs-hot-weather`
*   **Meta Title:** 5 Common Myths About Dogs & Hot Weather Busted | Dogfriz
*   **Meta Description:** Shaving double-coated dogs? Letting them lie on carpets after walks? We debunk 5 dangerous myths about how dogs regulate heat in the summer.
*   **Target Keywords:** shaving double coated dogs summer, how do dogs cool down, dog panting myths, dog summer care
*   **Article Structure:**
    *   **Introduction**: Highlight that well-meaning advice can sometimes put our dogs in critical danger.
    *   **Myth 1: "Shaving double-coated dogs keeps them cool."** *Busted:* Double coats act as insulation against both cold AND heat. Shaving them exposes skin to sunburn and traps heat.
    *   **Myth 2: "Dogs cool down the same way humans do."** *Busted:* Re-explain that dogs only sweat through paws and rely on panting.
    *   **Myth 3: "If they are in the shade, they can't get heatstroke."** *Busted:* High humidity can make shade ineffective if there is no airflow or fresh water.
    *   **Myth 4: "Ice water causes dog stomachs to bloat."** *Busted:* Clarify that cool water is completely safe and necessary, but extremely freezing water can cause rapid blood vessel constriction.
    *   **Myth 5: "Panting after a walk is just normal recovery."** *Busted:* Persistent, heavy panting indicates a high core body temperature that needs immediate cooling intervention.
    *   **Conclusion / Call to Action**: Direct users to explore our complete suite of safe, veterinarian-backed products.
