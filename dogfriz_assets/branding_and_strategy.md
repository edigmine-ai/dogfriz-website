## 2. Phase 1: Shopify "Clean" Setup Instructions

To get indexed quickly by Google, look authoritative, and remain clean of sales friction initially, follow these steps:

### Step 1: Install & Structure
1.  Sign up for Shopify.
2.  Choose the **Dawn** or **Sense** theme (clean, typography-driven, and fast-loading).
3.  Go to `Online Store -> Navigation` and set up your menu:
    *   **Home** (A clean landing page introducing Dogfriz)
    *   **Summer Safety Guides** (Your blog containing the 4 articles)
    *   **Canine Gear Science** (Your products listed as technical showcases—no buy buttons)
    *   **Our Mission** (About Us page focusing on animal welfare and summer safety)

### Step 2: "Hide" the E-Commerce Cart
To give your store a "clean, non-transactional" informational feel initially:
1.  Go to `Online Store -> Themes -> Customize`.
2.  In the header settings, uncheck or hide the **Cart icon** if the theme allows.
3.  **For Product Pages:** Instead of using default templates with a buy button, create a custom page template or use a Rich Text block that says: *"Our gear is currently undergoing safety and laboratory testing for the upcoming season. Read our scientific breakdowns below to understand canine cooling mechanics."*

### Step 3: Populate the Content
*   Create **Pages** for the 3 individual products and 2 bundles using the copy in `products/`.
*   Create a **Blog** named "Summer Safety Guides" and publish the 4 articles from `blogs/` using the specified headings, meta titles, and descriptions.

## 3. The Transition Strategy: Activating Commerce (Phase 2)

Once your blog is being crawled and indexed by Google Search Console, and your YouTube Shorts are generating solid referral traffic, it’s time to monetize.

1.  **Select Your Supplier:** Go to AliExpress, Zendrop, or AutoDS and search for:
    *   *Evaporative Dog Cooling Vest* (Match the three-layer style).
    *   *Pressure-Activated Gel Pet Cooling Mat*.
    *   *Portable Dog Water Bottle with leaf/bowl lid*.
2.  **Add Your Products:** Import the products into Shopify using your custom showcase descriptions. Add variants for sizes (S, M, L, XL) and colors (prefer light blue, gray, or neon orange for high visibility).
3.  **Turn on E-Commerce:**
    *   Re-enable the cart icon in your theme settings.
    *   Change the showcase pages into actual Shopify Product Pages with active "Add to Cart" and checkout buttons.
4.  **Launch the Bundles:** Use a free Shopify bundle app (like *Shopify Bundles*) to group the Vest + Flask (Summer Safety Kit) and Vest + Mat (Ultimate Cool-Down System) with automatic 15% discounts applied at checkout.
5.  **Change Your Video Call to Actions:**
    *   Update your YouTube Shorts descriptions and pinned comments from *"Read the full guide at dogfriz.com"* to *"Shop the Dogfriz Cooling Vest at dogfriz.com (Link in Bio)!"*
