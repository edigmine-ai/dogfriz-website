## Technical Overview

The **Dogfriz Core Cooling Vest** is designed as a physical thermoregulation aid to help dogs safely exercise and explore during high-heat conditions. Since dogs cannot sweat, they lack a natural mechanism to dissipate heat through their skin. This vest acts as an external, artificial sweat layer, using advanced material science to keep your dog comfortable and protected.

Whether you have an active trail runner, a flat-faced breed prone to respiratory restriction, or a senior companion, the Dogfriz Core Cooling Vest provides continuous, non-invasive cooling.

---

## Key Features & Materials

### 1. Three-Layer Evaporative System
*   **Reflective Mesh Outer Layer:** Engineered to block and reflect radiant heat from direct sunlight, preventing thermal buildup in the dog’s fur.
*   **Super-Absorbent Middle Layer:** Holds a reservoir of water without feeling heavy or soggy. This water is slowly released through evaporation over several hours.
*   **Wicking Inner Comfort Layer:** Transfers the cool sensation directly to your dog's chest, neck, and upper back (where vital blood vessels are closest to the skin) while keeping their fur dry.

### 2. Ergonomic Athletic Fit
*   Designed with a deep chest panel for maximum contact with the heart and lung area—the dog’s primary heat-exchange zones.
*   Constructed with flexible side panels to allow a full, unrestricted range of motion for walking, running, and playing.
*   Adjustable side buckles allow a secure, snug fit that keeps the cooling layer flush against your dog's body.

### 3. Integrated Harness Attachment
*   Features a dual-point zipper entry on the back, allowing you to easily attach a leash to any standard underlying harness.
*   Includes high-visibility reflective striping on the seams for safe walks during dawn or dusk "golden hours."

## Operating Instructions

1.  **Soak:** Submerge the vest entirely in cool water.
2.  **Wring:** Squeeze out the excess water gently until the vest is damp but not dripping.
3.  **Wear:** Fit the vest onto your dog and secure the adjustable buckles.
4.  **Re-Wet:** When the vest feels dry to the touch, simply pour a small cup of water over the top to reactivate the evaporative cooling process.
