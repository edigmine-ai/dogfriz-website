## Technical Overview

Proper hydration is the absolute first line of defense against canine heat stroke and physical exhaustion. During outdoor exercises, a dog's panting causes rapid fluid loss. If they cannot access clean, cool water immediately, their blood thickens, making heat regulation incredibly difficult.

The **Dogfriz Portable Hydration Flask** is designed for active pet owners who need a fast, leak-proof, and clean way to hydrate their dogs during hikes, walks, or park trips. It integrates a lightweight, durable water bottle with a food-grade silicone drinking leaf, allowing for effortless, one-handed operation.

## Safe Hydration Best Practices

*   **Avoid Puddles:** Natural puddles of standing water are breeding grounds for harmful bacteria, parasites (like *Giardia*), and chemical runoff. Always offer fresh tap or bottled water from your flask.
*   **Frequent Small Sips:** Do not let your dog chug massive amounts of water immediately after running, as this can lead to bloating or stomach twisting (GDV). Instead, offer small, frequent sips every 10–15 minutes during your walks.
