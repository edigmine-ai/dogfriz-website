## Technical Overview

The **Dogfriz Cooling Mat** is designed to provide rapid post-exercise recovery and comfortable resting spots for dogs indoors or in the shade. Dogs generate a substantial amount of internal metabolic heat during activity, which is difficult for them to release when resting on insulating carpets or standard pet beds.

This mat uses **pressure-activated gel technology** to absorb your dog's core body heat upon contact, providing instant, refreshing cooling. It requires no water, electricity, or refrigeration, making it a safe, automatic cooling solution that is ready whenever your dog lies down.

---

## Key Features & Technology

### 1. Pressure-Activated Gel Core
*   The cooling gel is formulated to react directly to your dog's weight. As soon as your dog lies down, the gel absorbs their body heat and dissipates it back into the surrounding air.
*   Provides up to **3 consecutive hours of cooling**, dropping the contact surface temperature by up to 10°F below ambient room temperature.
*   **Self-Recharging:** To recharge the gel, simply remove the dog from the mat for 15–20 minutes. The gel will automatically release the trapped heat and reset to its cool state.

### 2. Durable & Pet-Safe Materials
*   The gel is 100% non-toxic, pet-safe, and biodegradable.
*   The outer sleeve is made from thick, puncture-resistant 300D Oxford nylon, reinforced with double-sealed seams to withstand scratching, light chewing, and heavy use.
*   Features a water-resistant, wipe-clean surface for easy maintenance.

### 3. Highly Portable & Multi-Use
*   Folds easily into a compact square, making it perfect for car seats, crates, or bringing along to outdoor picnics and patio lunches.
*   Acts as an excellent therapeutic pad for older senior dogs suffering from joint pain or arthritis, as the cool surface reduces inflammation.

## Care & Maintenance

*   **To Clean:** Simply wipe down with a damp cloth, a mild soap solution, and let air dry.
*   **Storage:** Store in a cool, dry place. Do not leave the mat exposed to direct blazing sunlight for extended periods, as this can degrade the self-cooling properties of the gel core.
